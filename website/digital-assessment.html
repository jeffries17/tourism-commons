Assessment Framework

Every business is evaluated across six digital dimensions, each scored 0-10 points using clear, objective criteria:

📱 Social Media Presence

Platform presence, posting frequency, business features, engagement quality, and content strategy.

🌐 Website Quality

Functionality, mobile responsiveness, content freshness, contact information, and user experience.

📸 Visual Content

Photo quality, variety, recency, brand consistency, and professional presentation.

🔍 Discoverability

Search visibility, Google Business listings, directory presence, review management, and online reputation.

💳 Digital Sales

Online booking capability, payment processing, product catalogs, customer communication channels.

🔗 Platform Integration

TripAdvisor presence, booking platform integration, tourism directory listings, and partnership visibility.

Sector-Specific Weighting

We recognize that different business types have different digital priorities. A tour operator's success depends more on discoverability and platform integration, while a craft workshop might prioritize visual content and social media. Our framework applies sector-specific multipliers to ensure fair and relevant evaluation.
