Welcome back · 0:00–0:10

Today is one long conversation.

1
Question 1
20 min · open table

What do your members come to you for, most often?

We're not asking each of you to list yours. We're trying to spot the 3 or 4 things that come up across the table — the pattern.
2
Question 2
20 min · open table

What data do you already collect — or could collect — about your members or your sector?

Most institutions hold more data than they realise. Some sits in old project folders. Some lives in your head. Some you could ask for next week if you wanted to.
3
Question 3
20 min · open table

What content do you already have — sitting in folders, hard drives, WhatsApp groups, or drawers?

Photos from a 2022 festival. A short film no one watched again. A list of journalists from one project. What's in the building?
4
Question 4
20 min · open table

Think of a time you helped a member succeed. What did you actually do?

Often the thing you did was small — an introduction, a forwarded email, a seat at a meeting. Those small things are the institutional value already being delivered. Naming them out loud shows the room what's already happening.
5
Question 5 · the big one
30 min · round the table

From this week — what's one thing you'd like to keep going?

Something from the SME training, from yesterday, or from this conversation. Around the table, each of you names one — and answers three short questions: what do you need? who do you need to talk to? how would you actually do it?
We all leave with one way forward. Not a commitment. A direction — specific enough that you'd know on Monday what to do next.

Closing · one sentence each
In one sentence — your one way forward.
The thing from Q5 you'll actually move on. Said aloud, so the room hears it and the person you need to talk to knows you said it.
