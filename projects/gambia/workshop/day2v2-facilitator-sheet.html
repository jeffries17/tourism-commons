Day 2 v2 · Seed prompts & probes

For your eyes only. The slides project just the question and the frame. These prompts are here in case the room goes quiet.
How to use this. Each question gets ~20 min (Q5 gets 30). Open the question, let the room sit with it for a beat, then wait. If two or three people speak, you don't need the prompts. If it stalls past 30 seconds, drop one prompt into the room as an offer — "some institutions tell us they hear about photos, or about getting found online — does any of that ring true here?" — and let them take it or leave it.

Keep capturing on the flipchart in your own words. The aim is the pattern, not the inventory.
Q1 · Member asks 20 min · open table
What do your members come to you for, most often?
Frame on slide: "We're not asking each of you to list yours. We're trying to spot the 3 or 4 things that come up across the table."
Seed prompts
  • Help getting found online
  • Help with photos & content
  • Connecting with international operators
  • Responding to reviews
  • Understanding what visitors want
  • Getting into trade fairs or events
  • Translation & messaging in other languages
  • What to post on social media
Probes if it stalls
· "When a member calls or shows up unscheduled, what do they usually want?"
· "Is there one request you wish you could answer better?"
· "Are members asking different things now than two years ago?"
Q2 · Data 20 min · open table
What data do you already collect — or could collect — about your members or your sector?
Frame on slide: "Most institutions hold more data than they realise. Some sits in old project folders. Some lives in your head. Some you could ask for next week if you wanted to."
Seed prompts
  • Member directory or registration list
  • Event attendance numbers
  • Visitor feedback members forward to you
  • Member check-ins or surveys
  • Numbers from past projects
  • Things you could ask members for
  • Visitor profiles members see day-to-day
  • Complaints or recurring questions
Probes if it stalls
· "What do you collect once a year for a report and then not look at again?"
· "If a journalist called tomorrow asking 'how many SMEs work in your sector?' — could you answer?"
· "What would you ask your members for if you knew they'd reply?"
Q3 · Content 20 min · open table
What content do you already have — sitting in folders, hard drives, WhatsApp groups, or drawers?
Frame on slide: "Photos from a 2022 festival. A short film no one watched again. A list of journalists from one project. What's in the building?"
Seed prompts
  • Event photos and videos
  • Member testimonials or stories
  • Operator and journalist contacts
  • Sector event calendars
  • Reports that mention your members
  • Old brochures or flyers
  • Member directories
  • Social media archives
Probes if it stalls
· "When was the last time someone took photos at one of your events? Where are they now?"
· "What sits in your inbox from past projects that you could resurface?"
· "Is there a contact list you guard but rarely share?"
Q4 · Helped before 20 min · open table
Think of a time you helped a member succeed. What did you actually do?
Frame on slide: "Often the thing you did was small — an introduction, a forwarded email, a seat at a meeting. Those small things are the institutional value already being delivered."
Seed prompts
  • Made an introduction
  • Forwarded an opportunity
  • Let a member use your space
  • Brought a member to a meeting
  • Wrote a recommendation
  • Vouched for a member
  • Shared a contact list
  • Included a member in a project
Probes if it stalls
· "Is there a member you'd point to as a story you're proud of?"
· "What did you do that the member couldn't have done alone?"
· "Did it cost you anything? Time, a phone call, your name?"
Q5 · the big one 30 min · round the table
From this week — what's one thing you'd like to keep going?
Frame on slide: "Something from the SME training, from yesterday, or from this conversation. Around the table, each of you names one — and answers three short questions: what do you need? who do you need to talk to? how would you actually do it?"
The three questions each person answers
What do you need? Information, contact, content, a decision, time, a budget line — name it concretely.
Who do you talk to? A name or a role. Inside your institution, or outside. The person whose yes/no determines what happens.
How would you do it? The first concrete step — a meeting, an email, a phone call, putting something on a website.
Seed prompts — what they might continue with
  • Setting up a Google Alert for their sector
  • Doing the monthly TripAdvisor scan
  • Adding a member directory or gallery to their site
  • Making one introduction to an operator
  • Pulling old photos into a shared folder
  • Sharing a short note with members about trends
  • Opening one event to media or buyers
  • Co-hosting a small fam visit with another institution
  • Featuring members in their social channels
  • Bringing a member to a meeting that matters
  • Aggregating something members can't aggregate themselves
  • Continuing one specific thing from the SME training
Probes if it stalls
· "What from the SME training this week did you find yourself thinking about afterward?"
· "Is there anything you've heard this week that made you think — 'we already do something close to that'?"
· "If you had two hours next month, what would you actually do?"
· "Who in your institution would you need to bring this to?"
Close 10 min · one sentence each
In one sentence — your one way forward.
Each person, briefly. The thing from Q5 they'll actually move on. "We all leave with one way forward."
If the room is reluctant
· Start with someone you know will speak, set the tone.
· If someone repeats what they said in Q5, that's fine — say it back to them in your own words.
· No need for a flipchart at this point. Let it be spoken.
