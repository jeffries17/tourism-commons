# Global Birdfair 2026 — Gambia Trade-Fair Prep

**Source list:** Rutland Global Birdfair Exhibitors workbook (228 organisations across 46 countries).
**Methodology:** Two-pass triage. Pass 1 classified obvious non-targets from name + country signal alone. Pass 2 visited the website of every plausible trade buyer, regional specialist, conservation body and US operator (63 organisations) and produced full A/B/C/D classification blocks with priority levels and engagement goals.

## Category legend

- **A** — Existing Gambia Seller (verified active Gambia tour or programme)
- **B** — West Africa / Regional Birding Specialist (visible W Africa focus, even if not Gambia today)
- **C** — International Birding & Nature Tourism Operator (global/multi-region birding op without current W Africa focus — potential future fit)
- **D** — Institutional / Conservation / Media / Ecosystem Stakeholder
