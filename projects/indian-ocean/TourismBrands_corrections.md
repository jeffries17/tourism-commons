## Material corrections

- [ ] **[19] Pavillon Bleu — "only two French overseas territories" framing outdated for 2025** — The claim is accurate for 2024 beach-only data (only Réunion and French Polynesia held the distinction for beaches). However, in 2025 marinas in Martinique and Guadeloupe also received the label, so Réunion is no longer one of only two. Qualify the claim to 2024 data or rewrite to reflect 2025 reality.

- [ ] **[19] Saint-Gilles marina not new in 2025** — "In 2025 this extended to four beaches plus the Saint-Gilles marina" implies the marina was newly added. It already held Pavillon Bleu in 2024 (third consecutive year); 2025 was its fourth. Rewrite: all five sites (four beaches + marina) retained their labels in 2025.

- [ ] **[29] Eden Lodge "Gold" in 2011 is structurally impossible** — Green Globe requires 5 consecutive years of certification before Gold can be awarded, so a 2011 Gold is impossible. The 2011 certification is confirmed; Gold came years later. Remove "(Gold)" from the 2011 claim — write "first Green Globe certified hotel in Madagascar and the Indian Ocean region (2011)."

- [ ] **[31] Madagascar Tourism Expeditions Travelife** — Could not be confirmed from the Travelife member directory or the operator's own site. Either verify against the live Travelife directory before publishing, or remove the claim.

## Minor corrections / caveats to add

- [ ] **[15] "Over 40" vs "around 40"** — The cited mauritiusnow.com source uses "around 40" in some renderings and "over 40" in others. Change to "around 40" to match the more conservative phrasing, or verify the precise count against the GSTC Certified Hotels Directory.

- [ ] **[3] Vanilla Islands formal entity** — The initiative launched 4 August 2010 (confirmed). The formal legal Vanilla Islands Organisation (VIO) association was not constituted until July 2012. Add a footnote if precision matters for a legal/institutional readership.
